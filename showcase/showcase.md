# Coil

A bright serpent in a dark circuit. Coil keeps you moving at a steady pulse, threading a path through an enclosed neon grid. Every amber pellet feeds your chain and leaves a little less room for the next turn.

Plan ahead. You can turn across your path, but you can never double back. One touch of the perimeter or your own body ends the run, so every route needs an exit.

Speed between pellets is where the score lives. Quick pickups build your multiplier from x1 to x5. Let the window drain and the chain bonus falls back to one. The safest route and the richest route may pull you in different directions.

Steer with arrows, WASD, or the touch pad. Pause whenever you need a breath, then return to the exact circuit you left. Chase your session best, restart, and find a cleaner line.
